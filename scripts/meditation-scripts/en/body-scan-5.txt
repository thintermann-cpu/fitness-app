Begin by finding a comfortable position, lying down on your back... Allow your body to settle into the surface beneath you... Feel the gentle support of the ground... 

Take a deep breath in through your nose... Hold it for a moment... And now, slowly exhale through your mouth... Let your body relax deeper into the ground with each breath out... 

Now, bring your awareness to your feet... Notice them resting on the surface... Feel any sensations in your toes... Your arches... Your heels... 

As you breathe in, acknowledge any tension you might feel... And as you exhale, imagine that tension softly melting away... Let it drift away like a feather on the wind... 

Move your attention to your legs... Your calves... Your shins... Your thighs... Feel the weight of your legs... Notice if there is any tightness... 

Take a deep breath in... Hold it... And exhale, letting go of any tightness... Let your legs become heavier... Feel them sink into the ground... 

Now, shift your focus to your pelvis... Notice the sensation of contact with the surface... Is there any tension there? Breathe in... And as you breathe out, release any tightness... Allow your pelvis to relax into the ground... 

Bring your awareness to your belly... Feel it rise and fall with each breath... Is it tight? If so, breathe in... Hold... And exhale, letting go of any tension... Allow your belly to soften, feeling it gently expand as you breathe... 

Next, move your attention to your chest... Notice the rise and fall of your breath... Feel your heart beating... Is there any tightness in your chest? Inhale deeply... Hold for a moment... And as you exhale, release any heaviness or tension... Allow your chest to open and relax... 

Now, focus on your hands... Feel them resting at your sides... Notice any sensations in your fingers or palms... Are they tense? If so, breathe in... Hold it... And exhale, letting go of that tension... Allow your hands to become heavy and relaxed... 

Shift your awareness to your arms... Feel the weight of your arms resting on the surface... Notice any tightness in your biceps or shoulders... Inhale deeply... Hold... And now exhale, letting that tension slip away... Feel your arms grow heavier... 

Now, bring your focus to your shoulders... Notice if they are raised or tense... Inhale deeply... Hold... And as you exhale, let your shoulders drop... Feel them relax... Allow them to sink into the ground... 

Finally, bring your attention to your face... Notice any tension around your jaw, forehead, or eyes... Breathe in deeply... Hold for a moment... And as you exhale, let go of that tension... Allow your face to soften... 

Now, take a moment to scan your entire body... From your feet to your head... Notice how you feel... Is there any remaining tension? If so, breathe in deeply... Hold... And exhale, releasing that tension... 

Feel your entire body becoming heavier... More relaxed... More at ease... With each breath, you sink deeper into relaxation... 

Take a few more deep breaths... Inhaling peace... And exhaling any stress or tension... 

As you breathe, notice the rhythm of your breath... The way it flows in... And out... Allow yourself to be fully present in this moment... 

Let thoughts come and go... Acknowledge them... And then gently let them drift away... 

With each exhale, feel yourself becoming more grounded... More connected to the surface beneath you... 

Now, take a moment to appreciate this feeling of relaxation... This sense of calm that envelops you... 

When you feel ready, begin to bring your awareness back to the space around you... Wiggle your fingers and toes... Gently stretch your body... 

Take your time... There's no rush... 

In your own time, slowly open your eyes... Feel the light and the space around you... 

As you transition back, carry this sense of relaxation with you... 

Take a final deep breath in... And exhale... 

When you are ready, gently rise... And return to your day...
