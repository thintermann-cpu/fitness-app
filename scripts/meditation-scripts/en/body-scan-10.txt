Welcome back...

Allow yourself to take a moment to reorient in your surroundings. Feel the texture of the surface beneath you, the gentle support it offers. Notice the air around you, perhaps a slight breeze or the warmth that envelops you. With each breath you take, invite this awareness to settle deeper into your being.

As you breathe in again, visualize the air filling your lungs, nourishing your body, and then, as you exhale, imagine releasing any lingering tension or stress. Feel it dissipate into the atmosphere, leaving you lighter, more at ease.

Now, let’s return to the body. Bring your attention back to your feet. Feel the ground beneath you, the solid earth that supports you. Imagine roots extending from your feet, anchoring you firmly into the ground. With each breath, feel the connection deepen, as if you are drawing strength and stability from the earth itself.

Shift your focus to your legs. As you breathe in, visualize energy rising from the ground through your legs, filling them with warmth and vitality. With each exhale, feel any heaviness dissolve, leaving your legs feeling buoyant and free.

Now, allow this sensation to rise through your body. Let it flow up through your hips, filling your abdomen with lightness. With every inhale, feel that warmth expanding, and with every exhale, any tightness or discomfort flows away.

Bring your attention to your chest. Notice the gentle rise and fall with each breath. Feel your heart beating steadily, a rhythm that connects you to the world around you. As you breathe in, imagine drawing in love and compassion, and as you breathe out, release any self-judgment or negativity, letting it drift away like clouds in the sky.

Focus on your arms now. Feel the weight of them resting at your sides or in your lap. As you breathe in, imagine energy flowing from your heart down into your arms, filling them with warmth and light. With each exhale, let go of any tension, allowing your arms to feel relaxed and at peace.

Now shift your awareness to your neck and throat. Notice how they feel, perhaps a little tight or strained. As you breathe in, visualize a warm light enveloping this area, soothing and relaxing it. With every exhale, imagine any tightness melting away, leaving your neck feeling free and open.

Bring your attention to your face. Feel the small muscles around your eyes, your mouth, your forehead. As you breathe in, fill this area with warmth and relaxation. With each exhale, let go of any stress, allowing your expression to soften and your face to relax.

Now, take a moment to scan your entire body from head to toe. Notice how each part feels, the sensations flowing through you. Recognize the lightness in your limbs, the calm in your mind, and the peace in your heart.

With every breath, embrace the stillness within. Allow yourself to simply be in this moment, free from judgment or expectation. Here, in this space of tranquility, you are enough. You are whole. 

If thoughts arise, acknowledge them without attachment. Gently guide your focus back to your breath, to the sensations in your body. Let each breath anchor you deeper into this serene state.

As you continue to breathe, begin to visualize a soft light surrounding you. This light is your safe space, a cocoon of warmth and protection. Imagine it wrapping around you, shielding you from any negativity or distractions. Feel how comforting it is, how it nurtures your spirit.

In this moment, you are invited to simply exist. There is nothing to do, nowhere to go. Just be present with yourself. Allow this stillness to fill you, to envelop you. 

When you feel ready, begin to gently deepen your breath. Feel the air flowing into your lungs, filling you with life. As you exhale, let go of any remaining tension, releasing it into the universe.

As you prepare to transition back into your day, take a moment to express gratitude for this time you have given yourself. For the space to breathe, to relax, to reconnect with your inner self. 

When you feel ready, slowly begin to wiggle your fingers and toes. Bring gentle movement back into your body. Allow your awareness to expand to the room around you once more. 

As you open your eyes, carry this sense of peace with you. You are ready to embrace whatever comes next, grounded in your own tranquility.
